Let the selector train past the gate and export its tree as rules

The pipeline stopped at the Stage 1 gate every time, so we never actually had
a classifier to look at — no tree, no artifact, nothing to show a panel. Added
--force-stage2 so it trains anyway. The gate still runs and still prints its
verdict first; we just don't let it end the run. Added --quick too, for a
shorter demo run.

The tree now gets written out as JSON rules as well as a joblib, so it can be
evaluated without importing scikit-learn. That matters because scikit-learn
won't fit on the deployed instance. The export is checked against the sklearn
tree on every series and matches.

Fixed the old verdicts in main.py and the test docstring. They said the
classifier never varied across more than 2 of the 12 candidates, which was true
of an older version. It now recommends 5 of 12, and it fails earlier anyway, at
the gate. On held-out data it lands within 0.3% of the oracle, so the classifier
is doing about as well as its label allows — the problem is the premise, not the
model. All numbers are from synthetic series.
